# keys-sign: report (stopped at the 6:30 PM budget stop, Nov 13)

## What exists
- **Lettering in code** (`src/world/signs/lettering.js`): one set of stroke skeletons drawn three ways: FHWA-style highway alphabets (series B, C, D, E, E-Modified, mixed case, accents), a carved Roman with slab serifs (gold title face), and one-stroke casual brush lettering. No font files.
- **Painter toolkit** (`paint.js`, `motifs.js`, `util.js`): paints color and carving together (relief masks, distance-transform bevels, prismatic letters), then weathering (sun fade, chips to primer and wood, rain streaks, salt, droppings) and outputs albedo + normal + roughness/metalness and the cut outline. Motifs: roseate spoonbill, tarpon, queen conch, hibiscus, leaves, pelican, sea turtle, spiny lobster, palm, sailfish, anchor, lime slice, ribbon, carved text (the last seven are written but not yet used or looked at).
- **Welcome sign art** (`setpieces/keysSign/art.js`): our own layout (arched, coved board; half sun with carved rays; "Welcome to" arced over the sun; gold "The Keys"; tarpon leaping on the left; spoonbill in mangrove roots on the right; conch and hibiscus; "KEY PALOMA" ribbon; tagline). Baked at 4096 x 2304 to `public/assets/signs/keys_welcome_*` (about 5 MB) with `node src/world/signs/tools/bake.mjs welcome 4096`; back face `keys_back_*`. If the files are missing the game paints at 2K at load.
- **Set piece** (`keysSign.js`, `preview.html`): board cut to the painted outline, timber posts, braces, concrete footings, two arm floodlights (emissive face + lamp-system lamps at night), coral-rock bed, sea grape, three coconut palms, weeds, jersey barrier run with sloped end, W-beam guardrail, five utility poles with transformer and sagging wires, birds on the wires that scatter when the player comes close or on `shot`/`gunshot`/`explosion`/`horn`, mile marker, litter, cone, hubcap, tire marks. 57 draw calls, 23.5k triangles in the preview.
- **Placement** (`keysSign.v2.js`): reads `layout.setpieces.keys_sign`, then snaps to the nearest clear right-hand shoulder of the highway for traffic driving into the Keys (the layout point itself is on the road centerline). In the merged world it now stands beside the Overseas Highway on Key Paloma at about (688, 4639), 40 m along the road from the layout point (`docs/v2/shots/keys-sign/game_welcome_noon.png`). If the layout file or the land is missing it falls back to a temporary spot beside MacArthur Causeway on Watson Island.
- **Guide signs and blades** (`plan.js`, `draw.js`, `signs.v2.js`): pure function of `city.roadGraph`. 40 gantries / 83 panels in the merged world (29 / 59 on the old Miami-only graph) at motorway and trunk diverges, destinations found by following each branch downstream (first named road, district name from `city.districtAt`), real route shields (I-95, I-395, US-1, US-41, SR 836, SR A1A, SR 907, SR 887), exit tabs numbered along each route, EXIT ONLY bands, truss and monotube spans with catwalk and luminaires, column colliders. 1,732 street-blade posts in Miami-Dade style ("NE 8 ST", "Biscayne BLVD 800") with block numbers from the crossing street.
- **Check**: `node src/world/signs/tools/check.mjs` -> `docs/v2/shots/keys-sign/guide_check.json`: 59 of 59 on the old graph file; `game.signs.verify()` in the running game on the merged world: 83 of 83. The script reads `public/assets/miami/roadgraph.json`, which world-osm has since moved, so run the check in the game until the path is updated.

## Short of the bar / not done
- Welcome sign: only one art pass and one tuning pass; no side-by-side with ref_18 was made; morning-haze shot was taken but not looked at; night look was retuned once. Weathering, back face, bed, barrier, poles and litter were seen only in the wide preview shot, not inspected up close. Birds scattering was not exercised in a test. Plants are alpha cards and read as stylized next to the reference.
- Guide signs: exit numbers are sequential along a route, not the real Miami exit numbers; no advance-distance signs, no airport pictogram, no cantilever gantries; gantry width comes from the edge width, so on merged decks the columns may not sit exactly on the barrier line. Fictional-region names come from whatever the graph and `districtAt` return.
- Two exits at one I-395 gantry both read "Watson Island" (true, but a real sign would name the ramp roads). Ground around the Key Paloma spot renders flat and bare (other tracks).
- Blades: placed on the corner opposite city-look's own blade post so they do not stack; at some junctions both may still land on the same corner. Blade text was verified against the graph, not read from a screenshot.
- Not started: per-key chain signs, bait-shop and tiki-bar signs, regulatory and warning signs, highway mile markers, billboards, bend-on-hit posts, instanced small signs.
