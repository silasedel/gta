I'm making characters for a 3D open-world game set in Miami and the Florida Keys. For the rest of this chat, I'll send you
short character descriptions, one at a time. For each description, generate ONE image of that character with these rules:

- Full body, head to toe, standing in a clean T-pose (arms straight out to the sides, palms down, legs slightly apart,
  feet flat, facing the camera, neutral relaxed face, mouth closed, eyes open).
- Plain pure white background, no floor, no shadows on the background, no props in the hands unless I ask for them.
- Photorealistic but smooth and clean, like a modern high-end video game character render: realistic proportions, skin,
  hair and clothing materials, soft even studio lighting from the front, no harsh shadows, no dramatic lighting, no motion
  blur, no depth of field.
- Everything visible and separated: hands open with fingers slightly apart, hair not covering the face, clothes not
  covering the hands, nothing overlapping the body.
- No logos, brand names, text, numbers or real insignia on clothing or gear (plain or made-up patterns only).
- One character per image, centered, the whole body inside the frame with a little margin.

Reply with the image only. Ready? I'll send the first character now.
